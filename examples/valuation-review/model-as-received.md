## The argument in the room

The sponsor's deal team and the adviser spent the first investment committee session on
revenue growth. The deal team holds that 12% in FY26E and FY27E is a management case that
has never been achieved twice consecutively, and that 8% is the honest number. The adviser
holds that two OEM platform wins are contracted and that 12% is underwritten.

Nobody discussed the terminal block. The committee asked for a review before the second
session, with one question: *how much does the growth argument actually matter?*
